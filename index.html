How to weigh yourself
Weigh 2–3× per week, same days each week. Always first thing in the morning — before eating, after the bathroom.

Daily weight fluctuates 1–2 kg due to water and food. The 7-day rolling average is what matters. We'll suggest a calorie recalibration every 2 weeks if your trend diverges from goal.

1 / 5

Testing? Tap Bug

If something goes wrong, tap the Bug button and copy the report. Add what you expected, then send the error log to Stu on WhatsApp.

2 / 5

Set up Profile

Open the Profile tab first. Add your basics, goal, activity level, and bodyweight so Jot can show useful targets while you test.

3 / 5

Hold, speak, release

Hold the mic and try phrases like “oats, banana and whey” or “200ml milk”. Release to review what Jot heard.

4 / 5

Review and correct

Everything is editable before saving. Say “make that 150g”, “remove banana”, or tap any item to change it.

AI helps interpret unclear food logging intent, but Jot does not invent nutrition or save meals without review.

5 / 5

Jot gets faster over time

Use recent, usual, and repeated meals to log your real food in seconds.
